Child Safety Standards

Last updated 4 July 2026

Age restriction

soulyap is restricted to users aged 18 and over. Age verification is enforced server-side at account creation. Users who do not meet this requirement cannot create an account or submit content.

CSAM detection and reporting

Every piece of user-submitted content is automatically scanned for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) before it is stored or shown to any other user. This scanning runs on every submission without exception — it cannot be disabled, bypassed, or skipped in any environment.

If CSAM is detected:

  • The content is blocked immediately and is never stored or displayed.
  • A report is filed with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2258A. No account ID or personal information is included in the report — only the content hash and detection signal required by law.
  • The submitting account is flagged for immediate human review.

No content from minors

soulyap does not allow minors to create accounts or submit content. There is no mechanism for a minor to bypass the age gate. We do not knowingly collect any data from persons under 18.

In-app reporting

Every piece of content displayed in the app includes a visible report control. Any user can report a confession they believe violates our policies, including any content they believe was submitted by or depicts a minor. Reports are reviewed by our team.

Moderation pipeline

All user-submitted content passes through an automated moderation pipeline before reaching any other user. This pipeline runs server-side and cannot be bypassed by the client. Content flagged by moderation is blocked and never stored.

Human review

Flagged content and reported content is subject to human review. Accounts found to have submitted content that violates our child safety policies are permanently banned.

Contact

To report child safety concerns, contact us at nani.ajay@gmail.com. Reports related to CSAM are treated as the highest priority.
